Turks and Caicos is an archipelago made up of more than 40 islands and cays.

The islands are known for:

- Beaches
- Coral reefs
- Clear ocean water
- Resorts
- Local culture
- Dining
- Water sports
- Nature
- Relaxing vacations

Different islands offer different travel experiences.

Providenciales is one of the most popular islands for visitors and includes hotels, restaurants, shopping, beaches, and water activities.

Grand Turk is known for history, culture, beaches, and attractions.

North Caicos and Middle Caicos provide quieter experiences with natural scenery and beaches.

South Caicos offers coastal scenery and a more peaceful island atmosphere.

Visitors can enjoy activities such as:

- Snorkeling
- Diving
- Sailing
- Kayaking
- Boat tours
- Beach visits
- Exploring local communities
- Trying Caribbean food
