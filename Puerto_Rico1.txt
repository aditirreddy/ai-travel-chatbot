PUERTO RICO - One of my major goals when it comes to travel has been to take more trips to warm weather destinations and explore. I have explored beautiful winter destinations and gorgeous parts of the United Kingdom, but I definitely feel like I manifested this exact trip straight into my existence. It checks all of my boxes for this year: warm weather, beaches, open water, sun and so many things I’ve never tried or done before. I’m taking you along with me on my adventures to the Caribbean island of Puerto Rico. We’re going on a five-day road trip exploring the beautiful island and seeing so much of what it has to offer. This is my ultimate list of things to do in Puerto Rico, with a road trip map showing the locations of each place visited. Puerto Rico is presented as a destination for adventure, beaches, food, drinks, nature and trying new experiences.

PUERTO RICO HOTELS - Recommended hotels include Hotel Condado Vanderbilt (££), La Concha Resort (££), Copamarina Beach Resort & Spa (££), Hotel El Convento (£££), Parguera Plaza Hotel (££) and Tres Sirenas Beach Inn (££).

SAN JUAN - Spend the day in San Juan, the beautiful capital city of Puerto Rico, known for its colourful streets, kind people and excellent food. A food tour and walking tour around the Old Town is recommended, with stops at local spots to try Puerto Rican food while seeing the sights with a guide, learning fun facts, enjoying cocktails and discovering charming streets. The food tour was described as a 10/10 experience. A major food highlight was Taberna de Lúpulo, essentially a craft beer spot, where a Cubano sandwich and beer plus a passionfruit cocktail were enjoyed. The combination sounded strange but was described as amazing, and all the food and drinks were considered excellent. San Juan was described as such a cool way to explore a new place and part of a trip where you could practically fly to Puerto Rico just to eat and drink around the island. While travelling, staying connected is recommended, with Holafly eSIMs providing unlimited data for maps, finding restaurants and staying connected while on the move.

SANTA MARIA MAGDALENA DE PAZZIS - Visit the beautifully serene Santa Maria Magdalena de Pazzis cemetery in Old San Juan next to El Morro. The oceanfront cemetery is said to be located in a place where souls can move outward into the great beyond toward the sea, which was described as incredibly moving. It is also a historical landmark in San Juan and welcomes visitors to admire its beauty and the surrounding coastline.

BACARDI SAN JUAN DISTILLERY - Visit Bacardi’s large distillery in San Juan, described as being like Disneyland for rum lovers. A VIP experience included time mostly in the barrel room, which is normally very restricted, while regular visitors can book tours involving cocktail making or rum tasting. It was described as one of the best spirits and wine production tours experienced and highly worth visiting while in Puerto Rico.

EL YUNQUE NATIONAL RAINFOREST - Explore El Yunque National Rainforest, a short drive from San Juan, where a road trip takes you from sunny coastal views into a stunningly lush rainforest. Expect waterfalls, hikes and beautiful natural pools. Visit La Coca Waterfall, which is incredible and easy to reach. The rainforest is accessible by car, with parking available along roads or in lots near viewpoints. There are seven hiking trails with varying levels of difficulty, from very easy to more challenging, so comfortable hiking shoes are recommended and bringing a swimsuit is useful for swimming in the water. Stop at the Yokahú Observation Tower and climb to the top for beautiful views. For novice hikers, the Angelito Trail is recommended; it takes around 15–20 minutes and leads to natural pools and a scenic rope swing. Be prepared for rain because it is a rainforest, although the warm climate can make the rain feel lovely while exploring. Cameras and other equipment should be protected from the rain. A reservation is required to enter the forest, so visitors should book ahead through the official website. Location: El Yunque National Rainforest, Puerto Rico.

PONCE - Wander the streets of Ponce, Puerto Rico, the second-largest city on the island, known as the Pearl of the South. The beautiful buildings help explain the nickname. Take a walking tour of the city with Isla Caribe Walking Tours, which was strongly recommended, stop at the art museum and enjoy flavourful Piragua, or shaved ice, from street vendors.

GUANICA - Go snorkelling and swimming on a boat tour from Guánica. A boat tour with Scuba Dogs includes stops such as Gilligan’s Cay, approximately one mile off the coast of Guánica on the southwest coast of Puerto Rico. The experience includes swimming in shallow water, snorkelling and enjoying the beautiful conditions. The water was described as a dream and amazing to swim in. The trip strongly recommends doing adventure activities such as these when visiting a gorgeous destination like Puerto Rico.

GILLIGAN'S CAY - Visit Gilligan’s Cay, approximately one mile off the coast of Guánica on Puerto Rico’s southwest coast, as part of a boat tour with Scuba Dogs. The stop is ideal for swimming and snorkelling in shallow, incredibly clear and enjoyable water.

PUERTO RICO BIOLUMINESCENT BAYS - See one of Puerto Rico’s bioluminescent bays at night for a bucket-list experience. Algae in the water illuminates when touched, creating the appearance of stars glittering under the sea. There are three bioluminescent bays on the island of Puerto Rico and five bioluminescent bays in the world. The phenomenon cannot be properly captured on film but is described as stunning to the naked eye and something visitors simply have to experience.

SALINAS DE CABO ROJO - Visit the Pink Salt Flats at Salinas de Cabo Rojo in Puerto Rico. Although the area may look like a pink-water beach, it is actually a salt mine and a National Wildlife Refuge. Specific algae in the area creates the beautiful pink colour. It was described as a must-see Puerto Rico bucket-list destination and a place worth revisiting to spend more time exploring and taking photos and videos. Faro Los Morrillos Lighthouse is also a short walk away and was noted as a place to visit on a future trip. Visitors can safely explore and take photos but should stay on the paths, follow posted signs and note that drones are not allowed. Location: Salinas de Cabo Rojo, Puerto Rico.

BUYÉ BEACH - Lounge and swim at Buyé Beach in Cabo Rojo. The beach was described as stunning, with very clear and calm water and super-soft sand, making it perfect for spending a day lounging by the sea. Buyé Beach is located in Cabo Rojo, a beach town mostly visited by locals, and there are many other beaches in the surrounding area. Location: Buyé Beach, Puerto Rico.

RINCON - Go surfing in Rincón, Puerto Rico, a Surfside town described as the ultimate surfing destination and one of the best places to catch waves. The Domes beach was the location used for surfing and was described as perfect. Surfing conditions can change depending on the day, so booking ahead with a surf school such as Rincón Surf School is recommended because instructors can advise on the best and safest locations. The instructors were described as very nice and, for beginners, they teach the basics before going into the water to help with learning the waves. The experience was extremely successful, with the writer managing to stand up on the board every time. Surfing was described as an unforgettable, empowering experience, and the writer expressed a strong desire to return to the water. The trip was about trying new experiences, stepping outside of a comfort zone and saying yes to adventures. Location: Rincón, Puerto Rico.

HOTEL CONDADO VANDERBILT - When staying in San Juan, Hotel Condado Vanderbilt was described as a favourite, offering gorgeous coastal views and a very short drive to the centre of San Juan. It was described as having everything desired for a more luxurious stay and as an excellent way to start the trip.

COPAMARINA BEACH RESORT & SPA - For a more relaxed, beach-focused atmosphere, stay at Copamarina Beach Resort & Spa on the southwest part of Puerto Rico. The resort is directly on the water and includes beachfront property where guests can relax on the beach. Snorkelling and scuba diving are also available. This was the location used for the afternoon boat tour and was described as a good option for a more beachy and relaxed stay.

PUERTO RICO TRAVEL EXPERIENCE - The trip involved exploring Puerto Rico over five days by road, visiting San Juan, El Yunque National Rainforest, Ponce, Guánica, Gilligan’s Cay, the bioluminescent bays, Salinas de Cabo Rojo, Buyé Beach and Rincón, while experiencing food tours, historical sites, rum production, rainforest hikes, waterfalls, swimming, snorkelling, surfing and beach time. The overall message is to say yes to more adventures and try things outside your comfort zone. The writer was surprised by how much they loved surfing and felt proud of trying so many new things in Puerto Rico. Travelling to Puerto Rico independently to meet up with a group was described as empowering. The trip was part of a personal challenge to try more things that bring them out of their comfort zone and say yes to adventures.
