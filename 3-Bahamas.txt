THE BAHAMAS-With over 700 islands and cays, hundreds of beaches with white or pink sand, and an abundance of things to do, The Bahamas is one of the best vacation destinations in the world. The Bahamas is located northeast of Cuba and southeast of Florida. The best time to visit is from mid-December to mid-April, which is peak season when the islands are bustling with activity and the weather is at its finest. With warm weather year-round, The Bahamas offers beach days, tropical breezes, and many opportunities to relax or explore.

BIG MAJOR CAY-Big Major Cay, also known as Pig Island, is located in Exuma and is home to the original famous swimming pigs of The Bahamas. Visitors can interact with the swimming pigs as part of a wildlife experience. Many tours combine Big Major Cay with other Exuma attractions such as Big Guana Cay. An early morning tour is recommended for better lighting and fewer crowds. Full tours can cost around $200 per person.

ROSE ISLAND-Rose Island is a peaceful private island located near Nassau and is a popular destination for day trips. Activities include snorkeling, relaxing on the beach, and enjoying buffet-style meals. Some tours also offer overnight stays in treetop rooms or beachfront bungalows. A day trip costs about $130 USD.

STANIEL CAY-Staniel Cay is located in Exuma near Thunderball Grotto and is an important starting point for exploring several Exuma attractions. Thunderball Grotto is an underwater cave system near Staniel Cay that became famous after appearing in James Bond films such as Thunderball and Never Say Never Again. The grotto is a popular snorkeling and scuba diving site with marine life including sergeant majors, yellowtail snapper, and angelfish. Access to Thunderball Grotto is included in many tour packages.

BLUE LAGOON ISLAND-Blue Lagoon Island, also called Salt Cay, is located only three miles from Nassau and is a family-friendly and couple-friendly destination. Visitors can swim with dolphins, snorkel with tropical fish, and meet stingrays and sea lions. Other activities include Segway tours, sip-and-paint classes, and beachfront shopping. Prices start at $69 USD for adults and $45 USD for children.

EXUMA-Exuma is home to many of the most famous attractions in The Bahamas. Visitors can explore swimming pigs, turquoise waters, underwater caves, sunken planes, nurse sharks, iguanas, and beautiful beaches. Coco Plum Beach is a hidden gem known for soft white sand, crystal-clear turquoise water, and a peaceful atmosphere. During low tide, visitors may see sandbars stretching across the sea. Exuma also offers wildlife experiences, snorkeling, diving, and boat tours. Many of the attractions are spread across different islands, making tours and boats useful for exploring the area.

BITTER GUANA CAY-Bitter Guana Cay is located in Exuma and is home to the native rock iguanas of The Bahamas. Visitors can see the iguanas up close as they sunbathe along the rocky coast. The iguanas are accustomed to visitors, making this a popular wildlife experience. Tours often combine Bitter Guana Cay with visits to the swimming pigs and other Exuma Cays. The experience costs about $197.95 USD.

ELEUTHERA-Eleuthera is home to the Leon Levy Native Plant Preserve and the Glass Window Bridge. The Leon Levy Native Plant Preserve in Governor’s Harbour is a botanical sanctuary created to protect Bahamian plant life and showcase its cultural and medicinal importance. Visitors can explore shaded hiking trails, informative signs, and a medicinal plant trail. Admission costs $10 for adults, $8 for seniors, and $6 for children. The Glass Window Bridge is a natural bridge where the darker blue waters of the Atlantic Ocean meet the turquoise waters of the Bight of Eleuthera, creating a dramatic contrast. The bridge is free to visit, but visitors should be careful around wild waves and avoid the area during bad weather.

HARBOUR ISLAND-Harbour Island is located near Eleuthera and is home to the famous Pink Sands Beach. Pink Sands Beach is known for its crystal-clear water and light pink-colored sand created by crushed foraminifera shells mixing with white sand. The beach is free to visit and is a popular place for swimming, sunbathing, and taking photos. Harbour Island is also included in the Eleuthera Pink Sands Experience and Swimming Pigs excursion, where visitors can explore Pink Sands Beach, enjoy the island, and take photos at the wooden swings near the sandbar.

COMPASS CAY-Compass Cay is part of the Exuma island chain and is known for close-up encounters with nurse sharks. Visitors can swim with the gentle nurse sharks, which are harmless to humans but provide an exciting wildlife experience. The activity costs about $150 USD and is especially appealing to travelers looking for an adventurous experience.

NASSAU-Nassau is home to several popular beaches and attractions. Jaws Beach is located on New Providence and is known for shallow, crystal-clear water and very few waves. The beach was featured in the movie Jaws: The Revenge and is free to visit. Cable Beach is another popular Nassau beach that is ideal for relaxing and spending time on the sand. Visitors can bring their own food and drinks or visit nearby restaurants. The area also has resorts, restaurants, bars, and casinos. Junkanoo Beach is another free beach about a 15-minute walk from Nassau Harbor. Visitors pass through a busy shopping area and near the Nassau Straw Market on the way to the beach. At Junkanoo Beach, visitors can relax, ride horses, or visit bars such as Tiki Bikini Hut. Nassau also offers many excursions, including ATV experiences, catamaran cruises, historical tours, glass-bottom boat rides, and waterpark experiences.

NEW PROVIDENCE ISLAND-Cabbage Beach is located on New Providence Island and is considered one of the most beautiful beaches in The Bahamas. The beach is easily accessible and is great for relaxing and sunbathing. Visitors can also participate in water sports such as parasailing and jet skiing. Bars near the beach offer drinks and food. The water can become somewhat choppy on windy days.

LONG ISLAND-Dean's Blue Hole is located near Clarence Town on Long Island and is the second deepest blue hole in the world, reaching a depth of 663 feet (202 meters). It is surrounded by a natural rock amphitheater on three sides and a turquoise lagoon and white sandy beach on the fourth. The clear water and excellent visibility make it popular for snorkeling, diving, and freediving. The Vertical Blue Free Diving Competition is held here each year and attracts freedivers from around the world. Visiting Dean's Blue Hole is free.

NASSAU ATV, CULINARY & BEACH EXPERIENCE-This Nassau excursion costs from $269 per person and lasts approximately 3.5 hours, excluding transfer time. Visitors ride ATVs while exploring the scenery and heritage of The Bahamas, enjoy authentic local cuisine, cruise along Go-Slow Bend, try Guava Duff at The New Duff, explore Fort Charlotte, relax at Western Esplanade Beach, and experience the Arawak Cay Fish Fry.

NASSAU EVENING CATAMARAN CRUISE-This Nassau excursion starts at $127 per person and lasts approximately 2.5 hours, excluding transfer time. Visitors sail into the sunset along Nassau's coastline while listening to Caribbean music or relaxing as the horizon changes. An open bar and appetizers are available onboard.

REGGAE CATAMARAN CRUISE AND NASSAU BEACH BREAK-This excursion starts at $141 per person and lasts approximately 4 hours, excluding transfer time. Visitors cruise along Nassau's coastline aboard the Sol-Mate catamaran, pass historical landmarks and celebrity homes, snorkel at a vibrant reef, and enjoy a beach break and swim at Rose Island. The tour includes an onboard DJ, open bar, and appetizers.

NASSAU SPRING BREAK CATAMARAN CRUISE-This excursion starts at around $136 per person and lasts approximately 4 hours. Visitors cruise along the Nassau coastline with a live DJ, enjoy cocktails and dancing, and have the opportunity to swim at a local beach.

ELEUTHERA PINK SANDS EXPERIENCE AND SWIMMING PIGS-This excursion starts at $474 per person and lasts approximately 8 hours, excluding transfer time. Visitors spend time at Pink Sands Beach and explore Harbour Island before swimming with pigs on Meeks Patch Island, interacting with wild green turtles, and enjoying an island-style lunch at Sunset's Restaurant.

BAHA BAY WATERPARK AND BEACH BREAK-This excursion starts at $137 per person and lasts approximately 4 hours, excluding transfer time. Visitors receive access to the rides and attractions at Baha Bay and have time to relax at the beach. Private cabanas are available for an additional cost, and the waterpark has nine Bahamian dining options available for an additional cost.

TASTE OF NASSAU HISTORICAL EXPERIENCE-This excursion starts at $141 per person and lasts approximately 3.5 hours, excluding transfer time. Visitors explore Nassau's culture and history while trying Bahamian foods and drinks. Highlights include The New Duff, a rum tasting at John Watling's Distillery, Tasty Teas, and a cultural walk through Potter's Cay Dock.

NASSAU GLASS BOTTOM BOAT EXPERIENCE-This excursion starts at $103 per person and lasts approximately 2 hours, excluding transfer time. Visitors travel through Nassau Harbor on a glass-bottom boat and observe marine life and coral reefs without having to snorkel. The experience is especially suitable for families with young children or anyone who prefers to stay dry.

ROSE ISLAND HALF-DAY PIG INTERACTION-This excursion starts at $298 per person and lasts about 5 hours. Visitors explore Rose Island and interact with pigs that are different from the famous pigs of Big Major Cay. The tour also includes snorkeling in clear water, time at a beach, and a local lunch.

EXUMA FULL DAY SWIMMING PIGS POWERBOAT ADVENTURE-This excursion starts at $436 per person and lasts approximately 8 hours, excluding transfer time. Visitors explore several Exuma attractions by powerboat, including the swimming pigs of Big Major Cay, the Pablo Escobar Sunken Drug Plane, Bahamian Rock Iguanas at Allen's Cay, and nurse sharks at Compass Cay. The excursion also includes lunch at a local restaurant and leisure time at one of Exuma's beaches.
