VISIT TURKS AND CAICOS ISLANDS

Turks and Caicos is a Caribbean destination made up of many islands and cays. It is known for white-sand beaches, clear turquoise water, coral reefs, water activities, resorts, dining, and island experiences.

Popular attractions and experiences include:

- Grace Bay Beach
- Chalk Sound
- Snorkeling
- Scuba diving
- Boat tours
- Kayaking

- Fishing
- Visiting nearby cays
