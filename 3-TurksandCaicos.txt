TOP THINGS TO DO IN PROVIDENCIALES

Providenciales offers many activities for visitors.

Popular things to do include:

- Visit Grace Bay Beach
- Go snorkeling
- Go scuba diving
- Take a boat excursion
- Visit nearby islands and cays
- Go kayaking
- Try paddleboarding
- Go sailing
- Go fishing
- Explore Chalk Sound
- Visit historical attractions
- Try local food
- Shop in Grace Bay
- Visit different beaches

Water activities are especially popular because Turks and Caicos has clear water and coral reefs.

Boat tours can take travelers to nearby places such as:

- Little Water Cay
- Half Moon Bay
- Water Cay
- Fort George Cay

Little Water Cay is known for Turks and Caicos rock iguanas.

Visitors can also enjoy land activities, sightseeing, nature, and local food experiences.
